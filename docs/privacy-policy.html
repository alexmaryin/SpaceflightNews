ZERO DATA COLLECTION POLICY

Our Commitment

At Spaceflight News Explorer, we believe that privacy is a fundamental right. Our application is designed to be a pure information aggregator, providing you with the latest space news without any compromise to your digital footprint.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other identifiable information. Our app does not use tracking cookies, and we have no way of identifying who is using the application.

Usage Data & Analytics

We do not implement analytics tools that track user behavior, device information, or interaction patterns. The application functions entirely as a standalone reader for public news feeds.

Third-Party Services

The app fetches data from the Spaceflight News API (SNAPI) V4. While this involves fetching content from various external news sources, no user-identifiable information is shared with these providers during the request process.

Changes to This Policy

As our application evolves, we may update this privacy policy. Any changes will be reflected immediately on this page.
